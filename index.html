About Taqwa Education

Supportive tuition with clear academic goals.

Taqwa Education is a Birmingham-based tuition centre supporting children and young people at different stages of their education. Our aim is to build confidence, strengthen core knowledge and help each learner make steady progress.

We provide structured support in English, Maths and Science, alongside preparation for KS1, KS2 SATs, Grammar School entrance, KS3, GCSE and A Level studies.

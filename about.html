Who we are

A tuition centre rooted in the community.

Taqwa Education is a Birmingham-based tuition centre supporting children and young people at different stages of their education. Its aim is to build confidence, strengthen core knowledge and help each learner make steady progress.

Sessions run on weekends during term time, in small focused groups that allow tutors to give each child proper attention. The centre operates from the Yardley Muslim Centre (YMC) Building and shares its community-focused values.

Teaching Approach

Small groups, focused support.

Taqwa Education runs small, focused learning groups on weekends. This means each child receives proper attention, and tutors can adapt their approach to the learner in front of them — not the other way round.

Sessions are structured around key stages and exam milestones, with extra focus where it matters most: KS1 support, Grammar School entrance preparation, KS2 SATs, and targeted subject tuition for KS3, GCSE and A Level students.

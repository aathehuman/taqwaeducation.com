About Taqwa Education

Helping pupils build confidence and capability.

Structured tuition, clear goals and a supportive environment for learners at every stage.

A local tuition centre with a simple aim.

Taqwa Education is a Birmingham-based tuition centre supporting children and young people at different stages of their education.

Our focus is practical: strengthen core knowledge, build confidence, prepare pupils for important exams and help each learner make steady progress.

We provide structured support in English, Maths and Science, alongside preparation for KS1, KS2 SATs, Grammar School entrance, KS3, GCSE and A Level studies.

01

Focused learning

Small, purposeful sessions that keep learning clear and manageable.

02

Exam preparation

Targeted support for SATs, Grammar School entrance, GCSE and A Level work.

03

Community setting

Based at the YMC Building, bringing education into a wider community environment.

About the Classes

More than martial arts.

Karate is about far more than punches and kicks. Every session builds focus, respect and steady progress — skills that carry into school, home and everyday life.

  • Structured weekly sessions every Saturday
  • Suitable for complete beginners and returning students
  • Gradings and belt progression as students improve
  • Safe, supervised environment at the YMC Building
  • Taught with an emphasis on respect, discipline and effort
